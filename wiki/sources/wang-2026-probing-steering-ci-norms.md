# Do LLMs Know What Is Private Internally?

> [!warning] Seed page
> Metadata only, not yet ingested. Add `zotero`, and write the summary, when ingesting.

Probes open models for contextual-privacy norms grounded in [[contextual-integrity]]: reports that information type, recipient and transmission principle sit in separable directions, that models encode norms well yet still leak (a "privacy awareness gap"), and proposes CI-parametric steering (one direction per CI parameter, summed) as better than single-direction steering on CONFAIDE and PrivaCI-Bench. arXiv 2604.00209 (cs.CL).
