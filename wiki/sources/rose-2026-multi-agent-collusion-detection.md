# Detecting Multi-Agent Collusion Through Multi-Agent Interpretability

> [!warning] Seed page
> Metadata only, not yet ingested. Add `zotero`, and write the summary, when ingesting.

Detects secret collusion between LLM agents from their internal activations rather than their transcripts: per-agent linear deception probes whose scores are fused into group-level collusion tests (HELIOS), evaluated on a new benchmark (NARCBench) and on emergent collusion. arXiv 2604.01151 (cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA).
