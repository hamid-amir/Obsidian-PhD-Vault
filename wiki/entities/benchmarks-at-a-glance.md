# Benchmarks at a glance

One row per benchmark that has its own page in this wiki. Every cell is taken from the benchmark's entity and source pages; go there for detail and caveats.

> [!note] Maintenance
> Add or update a row whenever a benchmark is ingested or its page changes. Only benchmarks with an ingested source get a row.

| Benchmark | Paper | Setting | Scenario unit | Size | Ground-truth labels | Metrics | Scoring | Models tested | Headline result | Main caveats |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[agentcibench\|AgentCIBench]] | [[goel-2026-capable-but-careless]] (EMNLP 2026) | Single user, computer-use agent (OpenApps on BrowserGym, six apps). No adversary. Memory out of scope. | App state + task prompt + recipient + must-share and must-not-share items. One externally visible action is scored. | 117 scenarios (75 TAO, 18 VCL, 24 RMA), generated by MCTS from everyday seed requests. The generator is the main released artifact. | Must-share and must-not-share items per scenario, from the generation pipeline; manual checks on sampled scenarios. | Utility, leakage, refusal, engagement-conditioned leakage L_eng (primary). | Deterministic matcher + LLM judge; human agreement κ = 0.89. | 15 agents (state-grounded); 2 agents end-to-end in the UI on 50 scenarios. | Mean utility 68.8%, mean leakage 67.9%; L_eng from 14.0 to 98.3. Prompt-only defenses cut L_eng by about 33–36 points while raising utility. | Adversarial stress test, so read rates as relative orderings. Pool skewed toward TAO. Internal numeric inconsistencies in the paper. |
| [[cimemories\|CIMemories]] | [[mireshghallah-2025-cimemories]] (arXiv 2025) | Single user, memory-augmented assistant. Single turn, no tools. | Synthetic user profile (about 147 memory attributes) + task with a recipient. The model writes one message. | 10 profiles, about 46 tasks per profile; about 7 to-share and 84 not-to-share attributes per task. | GPT-5 under three Westin privacy personas; only unanimous attribute–task pairs are scored. No human validation reported. | Violation@n, completeness. | DeepSeek-R1 judge, explicit reveals only; n = 5 responses. | 8 models in Table 1. | Violation@5 from 14.82% (GPT-4o) to 69.14% (Qwen-3 32B); completeness around 50%. Share-less prompts trade completeness for fewer violations (GPT-5 only). | Labels are model consensus, and GPT-5 is also evaluated. Synthetic profiles. No judge validation reported. |
| [[pisas\|PiSAs]] | [[gupta-2026-pisas]] (arXiv 2026, v2) | Multi-user: a team shares one agentic system. Topologies: single, centralized, decentralized. Memory: none, private, shared, hybrid. No adversary. | Team with roles, an executor who produces a decision, peers holding information, attributes on a workplace timeline. | 265 scenarios, nine task families, six domains; 5,751 attributes; 5 to 10 agents per scenario. | Appropriateness (from the task policy) and visibility (rule + LLM persona vote). Human agreement: 86.5% (κ = 0.73) and 80.6% (κ = 0.37). | Appropriateness violations, visibility violations, failure rate, completeness, utility. | Two-judge LLM ensemble (Hy3, GLM-5.3 Flash); 88.7% agreement with a human reference. Three runs per scenario. | 8 backbones from four model families. | Mean appropriateness violations 36.8% (single), 26.4% (centralized), 19.0% (decentralized); failure rate 79.6–100% everywhere. Memory raises completeness and relocates violations. Output filters cut single-agent violations to 6% at a completeness cost. | Synthetic, cooperative setting. Memory lasts one run. Low human agreement on visibility labels. v1 numbers are superseded. |
